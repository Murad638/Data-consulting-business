Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What do I need to start?

One anonymised file and one business question. Tell me what decision you want to make; I will confirm whether the data is suitable.

Is the first sample free?

A limited first sample may be free: one file, one focused dashboard, and an agreed delivery date. I confirm scope and availability before work starts.

Do you need access to my systems?

Not at the start. An anonymised export is often enough. If ongoing integration is needed, we assess available interfaces and agree limited, read-only access where supported.

Can you work with tools we already use?

Yes, where suitable. We can start with CSV or Excel exports and choose the delivery format around your needs. API access and automation are assessed case by case.

How much does ongoing work cost?

Pricing depends on sources, complexity, update frequency and support. We agree on the scope and price before starting paid work.

How is customer data handled?

Please remove names, emails and addresses before sharing a sample. For real client work, we agree on data handling, EU hosting where applicable and deletion arrangements.

Start small

One file. One question. Real clarity.

Tell me what you want to understand. A limited free sample may be available; we agree the scope before starting. Share anonymised data only after we agree how to handle it.

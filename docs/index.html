The challenge for growing businesses

You have data. But do you have answers?

Small teams often cannot justify a dedicated data department. Valuable information sits in spreadsheets, sales tools and operational systems while everyday decisions rely on guesswork.

01 / Reporting

Hours lost to manual reports

Someone exports files, copies figures and fixes spreadsheets every week. By the time a report is ready, the numbers may already be out of date.

I help automate repeatable reports and check the data before it reaches you.
02 / Visibility

Revenue is visible. Profit is not.

Sales, product costs, returns and advertising live in different systems. You cannot easily see which products, customers or channels actually create value.

I connect relevant sources and build consistent metrics that explain performance.
03 / Decisions

Good ideas, unanswered questions

You want to test a new price, identify growth opportunities or understand why customers leave, but lack the tools or time to analyse your data.

I turn your business questions into focused analyses and practical next actions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A few things before we start.

What do I need to start?

One anonymised file and one business question. Tell me what decision you want to make; I will confirm whether the data is suitable.

Is the first sample free?

A limited first sample may be free: one file, one focused dashboard, and an agreed delivery date. I confirm scope and availability before work starts.

Do you need access to my systems?

Not at the start. An anonymised export is often enough. If ongoing integration is needed, we assess available interfaces and agree limited, read-only access where supported.

Can you work with tools we already use?

Yes, where suitable. We can start with CSV or Excel exports and choose the delivery format around your needs. API access and automation are assessed case by case.

How much does ongoing work cost?

Pricing depends on sources, complexity, update frequency and support. We agree on the scope and price before starting paid work.

How is customer data handled?

Please remove names, emails and addresses before sharing a sample. For real client work, we agree on data handling, EU hosting where applicable and deletion arrangements.

What support do you offer?

For ongoing plans, the proposed standard reply target is one business day; priority support can include same-business-day review of failed data loads. Exact hours and scope are agreed in advance.

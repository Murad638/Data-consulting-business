Solutions

Solutions for your business priorities.

Explore practical business questions across sales, profitability, operational reporting, customer activity, planning and billing. Each solution begins with the decision that needs support, then identifies the data and analysis required.

Business questions I can help answer

Different businesses need different insights.

Start with the obstacle, not a dashboard template. Each example describes a decision and the information needed to support it. The approach is tailored to the available data and agreed business definitions.

Sales & commercial performance

Identify which channels generate revenue, where leads are lost and how sales performance compares with targets. Use order records, channel information and funnel data to investigate growth opportunities.

Connect CRM and order data; track conversion, revenue against targets and opportunities.Explore example ↗

Product & e-commerce

Compare sales with product costs, returns, fulfilment and allocated advertising to identify products with weak or negative contribution margins. Review pricing, costs and product mix before scaling sales.

Combine product, order and cost records; identify contribution margin and return patterns.Explore example ↗

Operations & reporting

Identify repetitive reporting tasks and unreliable refreshes. Combine source data, automate repeatable steps and monitor failed runs and quality checks to improve reporting reliability.

Automate recurring data flows, add quality checks and monitor reliability and runtime.Explore example ↗

Finance & planning

Compare actual results with targets by period and business unit. Quantify variances and identify the areas that need investigation before adjusting a forecast.

Align business-unit definitions; compare actuals, targets and exceptions over time.Explore example ↗

Customer analytics

Analyse repeat purchasing and customer activity by segment. Where customer history is available, cohort analysis can distinguish repeat purchasing from retention and churn.

Combine transaction history and customer identifiers to measure repeat activity and, with suitable data, cohorts.Explore example ↗

Billing & reconciliation

Reconcile invoice records, payments and references to identify exceptions. Prioritise late payments and mismatches, using invoice amounts and due dates when available.

Combine billing and payment records, classify exceptions and prioritise follow-up.Explore example ↗

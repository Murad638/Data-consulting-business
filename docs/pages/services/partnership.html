Ongoing data partnership

Ongoing analytics and engineering support.

Access ongoing analytics, reporting and data engineering support without the commitment of a full-time internal data team.

How this service helps your business

After the initial project, an agreed monthly plan can cover data refreshes, quality checks, maintenance and regular business reviews. New questions and integrations are scoped together. Standard support has a proposed one-business-day reply target; priority review of failed loads can be agreed separately. Availability, response targets and costs are confirmed before work starts.

Potential deliverables

  • Regular updates and review
  • Support for agreed data loads and reports
  • New questions scoped together
  • Agreed support hours, scope and review cadence

Business questions we can answer

  • Who keeps our reports working?
  • Can we add a new source or metric next month?
  • How can reporting adapt as business priorities change?

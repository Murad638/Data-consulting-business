Data modelling

Consistent metrics and reliable data models.

Build reusable data models and consistent metric definitions so sales, finance and operations can work from comparable figures.

How this service helps your business

Different reports may define revenue, profit or active customers differently. I translate agreed business rules into reusable models, document assumptions and check consistency across sources. This makes future reports easier to maintain and reduces conflicting interpretations of performance.

Potential deliverables

  • Business rules and metric definitions
  • Reusable SQL or Python models
  • Checks for consistency and completeness
  • Documented assumptions, relationships and reconciliation rules

Business questions we can answer

  • What exactly counts as profit?
  • Can finance and sales use the same definitions?
  • How should discounts, refunds and costs affect reported profit?

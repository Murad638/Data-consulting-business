Data engineering

Reliable data engineering and integration.

Connect sales, finance and operational data from files and APIs into a reliable foundation for reporting and analysis.

How this service helps your business

Orders, payments, product costs and operational records often sit in separate systems. I connect the relevant sources, standardise formats and reconcile totals against source reports. A simple file-based process can evolve into automated API integrations where the benefits justify it. Storage may use existing infrastructure, a customer-hosted database or a dedicated DataProfit-managed solution where feasible. Hosting, costs and security responsibilities are agreed in advance.

Potential deliverables

  • Read-only source connections or exports
  • Clean database and data quality checks
  • Reliable data loads and documentation
  • Source-to-report reconciliation and documented refresh processes

Business questions we can answer

  • Can sales and costs live in one place?
  • Why do totals disagree between systems?
  • Can reporting use the same data across different systems?

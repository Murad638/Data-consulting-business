Privacy

Privacy & data handling.

Information about website inquiries, third-party processing and the arrangements for sharing data in a sample or ongoing project.

Website and inquiries

DataProfit does not install website analytics or advertising trackers. If you submit the inquiry form, FormSubmit processes the name (if provided), email, business name (if provided), topic and question to deliver the message to DataProfit. The service may use spam-prevention checks and its own cookies. DataProfit uses these details to reply and discuss your request; please do not include sensitive data or customer records. Hosting and email providers may process information as part of providing their services. See FormSubmit for service information.

Sample data

Please do not attach sensitive or personal customer information to an initial inquiry. For a sample, remove names, email addresses, addresses and other unnecessary personal identifiers before sharing a file. A customer ID may still be personal data if it can be linked back to a person. For ongoing work, the purpose, permitted access, storage location, retention, deletion and any required data processing agreement are agreed before data is transferred.

Questions

Contact DataProfit using the inquiry form if you have a question about data handling.

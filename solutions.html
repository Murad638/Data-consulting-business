What happens after you contact me

From the first conversation to a reliable data solution.

No need for a technical brief or a dedicated data team. We start with what matters to your business and grow only when the results justify it.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand your business

Tell me what you sell, how you work and what decisions are difficult. We discuss existing tools, reporting pain points and priorities.

02 / PROVE

Start with one question

We agree on a suitable anonymised Excel or CSV export and a focused sample. I confirm whether a limited free sample is feasible before starting.

03 / BUILD

Deliver a useful solution

Review the sample together. If it helps, we agree on scope, pricing and delivery. I can connect APIs, clean and model data, build dashboards and automate updates.

04 / IMPROVE

Keep your insights reliable

Where agreed, I maintain data workflows, monitor quality, refine regular reports and help answer new questions as your business grows.
